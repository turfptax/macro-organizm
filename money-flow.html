    How it works today: a loan creates the deposit

    A bank lends first and the deposit appears with the loan. Reserves are settled afterwards and are supplied by the central bank at its policy rate. The brakes are capital rules, profit at the policy rate and demand for loans. Each step uses a $100 example; steps 3 and 4 each start from the position after step 1.
