What this page measures

How fast do prices rise, which things rise faster, and what does it cost in work? Prices are counted here in dollars, in hours of work at average pay, and in ounces of gold. The page reports measurements and their limits. It does not say what prices ought to be or who is responsible for them.

  • MeasuredA number computed in your browser from the data files, with its period and source.
  • MethodHow a number is worked out, so that you can check it.
  • InterpretationA reading that economists dispute. Marked each time and never presented as a result.
